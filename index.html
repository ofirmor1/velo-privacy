🛡️ Privacy Policy for Velo

📅 Last Updated: September 14, 2025
🏢 Developer: kolt
📱 App Type: Free Service

🔐 Our Privacy Commitment

Velo is designed with privacy-first principles. We believe your data belongs to you, and we've built our app to reflect that commitment.

✅ What We DON'T Do:
• No data collection or tracking
• No analytics or usage statistics
• No personal information storage
• No location tracking
• No advertising networks
• No third-party data sharing

🔍 Information We Access

Velo operates with minimal data access:

Google Authentication

• We use Google Sign-In solely for YouTube API access
• Your Google credentials are never stored on our servers
• Authentication is handled securely by Google's systems
• We only request YouTube read access permissions

YouTube Data

• We access only the YouTube videos you choose to watch
• No viewing history is stored or tracked
• All video data remains on your device
• We don't access your YouTube playlists or subscriptions

📱 Device Information

The app may access basic device functionality for proper operation:

• Internet Connection: Required for YouTube video streaming
• Screen Overlay: For Picture-in-Picture and Ghost Mode features
• Audio Playback: For background audio functionality
• Notification Access: For media controls

Important: None of this information is collected, stored, or transmitted.

🔗 Third-Party Services

Velo integrates with the following services:

Google Services:
• Google Play Services - For app distribution and updates
• YouTube API - For video access and playback

Note: We don't use Firebase, Google Analytics, or any tracking services.

👶 Children's Privacy

Velo does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age. If you're a parent and believe your child has used our app, please contact us immediately.

🛡️ Data Security

Since we don't collect personal data, there's minimal security risk. However:

• All communications use HTTPS encryption
• YouTube access is limited to official YouTube domains only
• App permissions are minimal and clearly justified
• Regular security updates are provided

🗑️ Data Deletion

Since no personal data is stored, there's nothing to delete from our servers. Simply uninstalling the app removes all local data.

🌍 International Users

Velo is available worldwide. We comply with international privacy regulations including GDPR, CCPA, and other applicable laws.

📜 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be:

• Posted on this page with an updated date
• Communicated through app updates if significant
• Effective immediately upon posting

✅ Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal data, traditional data rights (access, deletion, portability) don't apply. However, you always have the right to:

• Stop using the app at any time
• Revoke YouTube access permissions
• Contact us with privacy concerns
• Request clarification about our practices
